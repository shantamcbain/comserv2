  LET’S TAKE A MOMENT TO TALK ABOUT THE TPU WHEELS.
  AFTER SEVERAL TESTS, I FOUND THAT A SETUP WITH 3 WALLS AND 25% INFILL WORKS WELL TO
  SUPPORT TWO MODULES, A DRAWER, AND AN X1C.

  NOW, LET’S BE CLEAR—THERE’S ABOUT 1 CM OF SIDE-TO-SIDE SWAY IN THE STRUCTURE. BUT THIS
  SWAY ACTUALLY HELPS ABSORB VIBRATIONS, PREVENTING THEM FROM SPREADING TO THE AMS.

  IF YOU'RE LOOKING FOR SOMETHING MORE RIGID, YOU’LL DEFINITELY NEED TO EXPERIMENT BY
  INCREASING THE INFILL PERCENTAGE OR THE WALLS NUMBER.

  HERE’S MY RECOMMENDATIONS:
  • IF YOU HAVE THE SAME CONFIGURATION AS MINE, INCREASE THE PERIMETER WALLS TO 4 AND
    THE INFILL TO 35%.
  • AS A GENERAL RULE, FOR EVERY ADDITIONAL MODULE YOU ADD, INCREASE THE INFILL BY
    ABOUT 8%.
